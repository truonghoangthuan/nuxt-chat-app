# Asset credits & licenses — courtyard v2

Production integration: all asset bytes remain unchanged from the approved preview. The environment, human IK, asset loading and renderer lifecycle are separated under `app/utils/incense/`; Vue owns the interface and authenticated history. The old `app.js` references below describe the original authoring source, not a shipped standalone runtime. Three.js 0.180.0 remains pinned; its complete MIT license is also served at [THREE-LICENSE.txt](THREE-LICENSE.txt). No preview localStorage history or sample identity is used in production.

No paid assets, purchases, sign-ins, external generation services, or protected downloads were used. All runtime resources are served locally. Environment architecture, foliage geometry, stone microtexture, smoke texture, interface, and ritual motion are original work for this preview; the person is a downloaded finished rigged asset, not a procedural mannequin.

## Reference vs implementation

The paid Sketchfab reference supplied by the user informed the preference for an anatomically proportioned clothed human only. Its model, textures and animations were not downloaded or copied. The courtyard is an original, generalized Vietnamese courtyard interpretation, not a survey/reconstruction of an identifiable religious institution.
